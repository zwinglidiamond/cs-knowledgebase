FAQ Articles10 articles
How do I process an external refund (PayPal, Venmo, wire, check)?Refund+
All external refunds require approval from Marge Laureola.
  • PayPal: collect email. Venmo: collect @handle. Wire: bank name, account #, type. International wire: also SWIFT code. Check: full name + mailing address
  • Go Green = exactly 50% refund — never full unless customer explicitly declines
  • Always verify the refund was actually completed — Ashley may confirm without acting
  • Never refund Mystery Gift or original order unless specifically requested
What do I do when a customer reports an adverse reaction to a product?Product+
Do NOT offer retention. Cancel sub AND cancel/refund order.
  • Collect: symptoms, when started, product, dosage
  • Physician involved → flag urgent, escalate to Ryan Bailis immediately
  • Order unfulfilled → cancel. Delivered → full refund
  • Document for QA review
→ Sample ticket
What is the correct retention offer when a customer wants to cancel?Policy+
Offer both options and let the customer choose:
  • Option 1: Pause subscription up to 6 months
  • Option 2: 20% lifetime discount — never offer 35% (Ashley error)
  • Adverse reactions: no retention, cancel and refund immediately
Ashley cancelled the wrong thing — order instead of subscription.AI Issue+
  • Check if cancellation can be reversed in Shopify / Fulfil
  • If fulfilled, process replacement if customer still wants product
  • Report in #cs-ai-issue-reporting, tag Enrico Dela Rosa
→ Sample ticket
A customer account cannot be found or a charge is not showing.Account+
  • Filter by last 4 card digits + billing address in Kustomer
  • Cross-check CheckoutChamp (CC) if Kustomer returns nothing
  • Still not found: ask for transaction ID or bank statement screenshot
  • Refund to different card → escalate to Ryan (possible unauthorized change)
→ Sample ticket
What are Ashley's known product information errors?AI Issue+
Always correct the customer and report each one:
  • 7-in-1 Blood Sugar: says NOT liquid — it IS liquid
  • 5-in-1 Vitamin B: offers capsules — capsules do not exist
  • Vitamin D3+K2: says bottles — it comes in pouches
  • Turmeric Curcumin 95: says 90 caps — only 30
  • Vitamin D3+K2: offers "Black" & "GLP" versions — only 1 version exists
  • Pause limit: says 3 months — actual limit is 6 months
Report all to #cs-ai-issue-reporting, tag Enrico Dela Rosa.
Customer received wrong item or wrong label (e.g. Toplux).Product+
  • Request photo immediately
  • Toplux label on True Nutra products → escalate to Ryan
  • Never use "Resend" — escalate CS for partial replacements
  • Discontinued TN011 & TN013 → replace with Magnesium Complex + Vitamin B Complex
→ Sample ticket
We received a legal letter, BBB inquiry, or trademark complaint.Legal+
  • Never respond directly — escalate to Ryan Bailis immediately
  • BBB inquiries: assign to Angel for drafting
  • Copycat sites (trytruenutrawellness.com, truenutra.co.za): report to Ryan and Mo
  • Do not close until legal team confirms resolution
→ Sample ticket
A customer wants to reach Coach Tom — what options are available?Policy+
  • Default: SMS / WhatsApp via number on file
  • International (Portugal, Germany, etc.): SMS not available — email is the alternative
  • Customer requests Coach Tom by name → assign to Enrico Dela Rosa
→ Sample ticket
The Go Green 50% refund is not processing correctly in Fulfil.Tech+
  • Ashley may escalate because Fulfil lacks "Plant a Tree" option — known UI gap
  • The 50% refund can still be processed — "Plant a Tree" is handled separately
  • Always verify total order amount before processing to ensure exactly 50%
→ Sample ticket

Policy Quick Reference
Rules Every Agent Must Know
🔁
Go Green Refund
Always exactly 50% — customer keeps product, tree planted in their name. Never full refund if customer agreed to Go Green.
⏸
Subscription Pause
Up to 6 months max — not 3. Ashley incorrectly says 3 months. Always correct this.
💸
Retention Discount
20% lifetime max. Ashley has offered 35% — this is incorrect. Never exceed 20%.
🚨
Adverse Reactions
Cancel sub AND cancel/refund order. No retention offers. Physician involved → urgent escalation to Ryan.
📦
Partial Replacements
Never use "Resend" — it ships everything. Escalate to CS for partial replacements only.
⚖️
Legal Letters & BBB
Never respond directly. Escalate to Ryan immediately. Make no commitments or statements.
🤖
Reporting AI Errors
All Ashley/Iris errors → #cs-ai-issue-reporting + tag Enrico Dela Rosa + link the ticket.
🌱
Discontinued Products
TN011 (Beef Organ) and TN013 (Hair Loss) are discontinued. Replace with Magnesium Complex + Vitamin B Complex.
📞
Callback Policy
We do NOT offer callbacks. Ashley has promised this incorrectly. Always correct the customer.
🛡️
Suspicious Emails
Shopify emails only come from @shopify.com — never Gmail or other domains.
